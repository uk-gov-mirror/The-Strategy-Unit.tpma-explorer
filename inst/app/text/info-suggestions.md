This app is in continuous development.
You can provide general feedback by clicking 'Give feedback' in the top-right.

[The Strategy Unit](https://www.strategyunitwm.nhs.uk/) has established a formal process to review, update, and maintain the set of TPMAs used in this app and [the New Hospital Programme Demand Model](https://www.strategyunitwm.nhs.uk/new-hospital-programme-demand-model).
If you have any thoughts about improving an existing TPMA or adding a new TPMA, then please get in touch and we will feed your comments and ideas into this process.
