The current evidence base means that the TPMAs are broad in definition.
While the activity shown in the tool has been classified as 'potentially mitigatable', no-one would suggest that all such activity can or should be mitigated.

A judgement is required of what proportion of the activity could be reduced.
It should be informed by the knowledge and experience of those involved, alongside available evidence.

The tool supports this judgement in two ways:

1. It can help local systems explore variation in hospital utilisation through comparison with statistical peers and the national average.
1. It presents the results of [an expert elicitation exercise](https://bmjopen.bmj.com/content/14/10/e084632) where subject-matter experts predicted how much activity would be mitigated nationally by 2039/40.

Note that some patient spells will fall into more than one category, so the TPMA opportunities may overlap.
