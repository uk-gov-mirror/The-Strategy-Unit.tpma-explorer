The Government's [10-Year Plan for Health](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/10-year-health-plan-for-england-fit-for-the-future) sets an ambition to reduce reliance on hospitals and deliver care closer to where people live. But which forms of hospital care might be avoided or transferred into community settings? And how much hospital activity can be avoided?

By reviewing the evidence, talking to clinicians, and analysing data, we have identified 34 Types of Potentially-Mitigatable Activity (**TPMAs**). These include care delivered in <span class="badge rounded-pill" style="background-color:#330072;color:white;">Inpatients</span> wards, <span class="badge rounded-pill" style="background-color:#00A499;color:white;">Outpatients</span> clinics and <span class="badge rounded-pill" style="background-color:#ED8B00;color:white;">Accident & Emergency</span> departments.

This activity can be mitigated through four **mechanisms**: de-adoption, hospital efficiency, prevention and redirection/substitution.

This tool allows you to explore these TPMAs: to see the level of these activities in your area or hospital, or examine trends and geographical variation.
